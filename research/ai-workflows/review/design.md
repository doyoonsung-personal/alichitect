**The three changes I would make first**

1. **Fix the semantic contract before committing to the layout.** Normalize the 24 samples and require explicit forks/joins, repeat groups, retry exhaustion, approval outcomes and side-effecting tool calls. These can remain lightweight design semantics; they do not require an execution engine or nested canvases.

2. **Replace percentile promises with an editable scoping estimate.** Define “one run,” expose workload multipliers, distinguish priced subtotal from total cost, and show baseline/stress scenarios. Include business success criteria, integration ownership, data boundaries and service targets. This is the material a pre-sales engineer would otherwise assemble by hand.

3. **Ship one complete, narrow sales flow.** Start with text-based support triage/RAG: stream the diagram, show approve/edit/reject and a bounded refinement loop, allow review of assumptions, provide step-to-runtime mapping and a shareable scope summary. Keep model chips secondary to business steps. Defer generic multi-agent design, voice, multimodal pricing, statistical latency, independently generated deployment synchronization and multi-diagram PDF work. The release is worth shipping when an engineer can use its output to agree a pilot’s scope and acceptance criteria with a customer.
