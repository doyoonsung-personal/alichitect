## A. SEED CATALOG (176 lines, current international site, checked 2026-10-31)
Format: category | canonical name | slug | one-line purpose | notes
Notes start with '<tier>/<place>'.
- tier: compute, container, storage, data, analytics, ai, ai-data, ai-security, integration, ingress, edge, network, hybrid, security, identity, governance, ops, media, comms, euc, saas, tool.
- place:
  - global, edge: outside any region.
  - region: regional managed service; draw OUTSIDE the VPC.
  - vpc: inside a VPC/vSwitch.
  - vpc-edge: on the VPC boundary.
  - attached: a badge on another node, not a node.
  - account: governance band.
  - onprem, client.
  - tool: not a runtime node.
