## What the plan gets right

- A separate workflow document fits the existing product-only cloud graph and avoids weakening its contract.
- Explicit deployment generation gives the user control over the extra request and its changes.
- Separate op names and an `ai` field are compatible with the current grammar and streamed-key restrictions.
- The early layout/routing spike targets a real risk. It should follow a small provisional placement contract.
- App-computed costs, design-model allowlisting and step-keyed proposal explanations are sound directions.
- Reusing the existing turn route preserves quota, locking, cancellation and redaction infrastructure, provided document identity reaches every execution path.
